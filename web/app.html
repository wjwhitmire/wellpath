Method and limits

Minimum curvature

Each course between two surveys is modeled as a circular arc in the plane of the two survey directions (SPE / ISCWSA industry standard). With inclination I and azimuth A at the upper (1) and lower (2) stations and course length dMD:

beta = arccos( cos(I2 - I1) - sin I1 sin I2 (1 - cos(A2 - A1)) ) RF = (2 / beta) tan(beta / 2), RF = 1 when beta < 1e-7 rad dN = dMD/2 (sin I1 cos A1 + sin I2 cos A2) RF dE = dMD/2 (sin I1 sin A1 + sin I2 sin A2) RF dTVD = dMD/2 (cos I1 + cos I2) RF

The dogleg is computed in the equivalent form beta = 2 atan2(|t2 - t1|, |t2 + t1|) on the unit tangent vectors t, which is exact for the same geometry and keeps full precision for tiny doglegs where arccos near 1 loses digits.

Rates, section and closure

DLS = beta(deg) / dMD x C C = 100 ft or 30 m Build = (I2 - I1) / dMD x C Turn = shortest signed (A2 - A1) in (-180, 180] / dMD x C VS = N cos(VSaz) + E sin(VSaz) Closure distance = sqrt(N^2 + E^2), closure azimuth = atan2(E, N)

Each rate is reported on the lower station of its course. Turn is shown as n/a (blank in the CSV) when either station of the course is within 3 degrees of vertical, where azimuth readings are unreliable and azimuth change per course length is not a steering number. DLS is still reported there. Interpolation at an MD inside a course follows the same circular arc: the direction is the spherical interpolation of the two end directions, and the position comes from a minimum curvature step from the upper station.

Assumptions and limits

  • Azimuths are taken as given. Wellpath applies no magnetic declination, grid convergence or north reference conversion; enter all surveys in one reference.
  • No survey tool error model, uncertainty ellipses or anti-collision. Positions are the nominal path only.
  • The unit toggle changes the course length for rates (100 ft or 30 m) and the labels. It does not convert numbers.
  • Default tie-in is the surface: MD 0, TVD 0, N 0, E 0, vertical. If the first survey is deeper than MD 0, the hole from surface to it is assumed to follow a minimum curvature arc from vertical (TVD = MD for a vertical first survey). Surveys above a user tie-in MD are ignored.
  • If the tie-in MD matches a survey, that survey's inclination and azimuth are used at the tie-in. Otherwise the tie-in is taken as vertical.
  • Measured depth must strictly increase. Inclination must be 0 to 180 degrees. Azimuth is normalized to [0, 360). Two consecutive surveys pointing in opposite directions (dogleg near 180 degrees) are rejected.
  • Turn rate is the azimuth change per course length. Near vertical, azimuth readings swing widely, so turn is not reported below 3 degrees inclination. The DLS a large azimuth change produces at low inclination can still look large while the hole barely moves; read it against inclination.
  • Minimum curvature is exact for a constant-curvature arc between stations. For real paths the error depends on survey spacing; closer surveys give a better path.
  • Both charts are drawn at equal scale on both axes. Section view plots TVD increasing downward.
  • Limits: 5 MB per file and 50,000 survey rows.
